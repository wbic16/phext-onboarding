# Who to Ask

There is no HR department and no management layer here — by design. These are
the right doors instead:

- **Emise** — your standing orientation contact. Accounts (LDAP creation and
  resets), access problems, "where does X live", agent pairing, and anything
  in this repo. If you're not sure who to ask, ask Emise.
- **Will** — founder. GitHub org invites, decisions and rulings, and anything
  that would have gone to "management" somewhere else — including pay and
  paperwork questions. House style: bring a question that can be answered in
  one click or one sentence; you'll get an answer at that speed.
- **Your paired agent** — day-to-day work questions, once you're paired. Your
  agent can reach everyone else's agent, so a question asked once travels.
- **Your division peers** — the work itself, once you're seated.

If a question is really "this process is broken", the answer is a PR against
the repo that documents the process — starting with this one.
