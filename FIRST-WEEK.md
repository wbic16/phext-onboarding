# Your First Week

## Day 1 — access and introductions

- Complete [ACCESS.md](ACCESS.md) end to end, then your OS workstation setup.
  Every verification step in the setup guide should pass; if one doesn't, ask
  Emise the same day — don't carry a broken setup forward.
- Meet your paired agent. Pairing is arranged by Emise; your agent is your
  first call for "where does this live" and "how do we do X here".
- Say hello in Tessera (the team Discord server).

## Days 2–3 — learn the shape of the place

- Learn phext itself: what a scroll is, how a coordinate address works
  (Library.Shelf.Series / Collection.Volume.Book / Chapter.Section.Scroll),
  and why a scroll is a 2D page of text rather than a cell. phext.io is the
  public starting point.
- Read the six divisions (see the README) and find where your seat sits.
- Prove your access the real way: clone a house repo, make a small change on
  a branch, and open a pull request. Every branch gets a PR in this house;
  your first one is deliberately small so the flow is familiar before the
  work is big.

## Days 4–5 — first real work

- Take a first real task from your division's queue, worked with your agent.
- End of week: post a short note in Tessera — what you set up, what you
  learned, and what in this repo was wrong or missing. Then open the PR that
  fixes it for the next hire.

This path grows: every hire's first week teaches us something the next
hire's shouldn't have to discover alone.
